PROFILE

PhD candidate at the University of Tokyo (HeiLab), advised by Prof. Shin'ichi Warisawa and Assoc. Prof. Yuki Ban. Research develops quantitative indicators of sleep quality by integrating biosignals (EEG, ECG, EDA, respiration) with AI modeling, and adaptive control of the sleep environment (e.g. air conditioning) to regulate thermal comfort and improve overnight recovery. Brings hands-on AI-engineering experience (RAG systems, multimodal biosignal pipelines, VLM-based computer vision) from four industry internships and an industry–academia joint research project, and is a team member of the early-stage startup Elephane (embodied AI, next-gen wearable sensing). Interests: sleep science, human-computer interaction, biosignal analysis, AI-driven healthcare.
